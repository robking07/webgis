- **D-12 · 2026-09-30 · DEFAULTED** · **Ramp accessibility thresholds and two ramp fixes.** The check (`packages/design/scripts/check.ts`) simulates protan/deutan/tritan vision at full severity (Machado 2009) and uses ΔE2000. Thresholds: adjacent sequential/diverging classes ≥ 5, categorical pairs ≥ 10, bivariate pairs ≥ 8. ColorBrewer's 7-class sequential steps are about 5–8, so 5 means "clearly perceptible", not "far apart"; tighten it if you want. Two fixes came out of the first run: (1) YlGnBu certified to 6 classes, because at 7 its protan adjacent ΔE is 4.7; (2) Stevens' blue-pink bivariate failed protan (5.9), and none of the other published 3×3 schemes I tested passed all three CVD types. Replaced with a project-generated blue × gold grid (OKLab bilinear, searched for the largest closest-pair distance, min ΔE 16.6 under every simulation). Not a §10 stop: a safe alternative exists.
